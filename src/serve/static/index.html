Limits

  • Clips of up to 30 s only. No punctuation or capitals.
  • Somewhat worse than the original Whisper-small on read speech such as audiobooks.
  • Each accent group had only 2 to 6 test speakers, so per-accent results are rough.
  • Not for medical, legal or other high-stakes use, or as unreviewed captions.
